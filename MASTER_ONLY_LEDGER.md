- **applies**: whether the commit's patch applies as text to `callgrind-20g`, after every earlier commit that applied. `yes`, `no` (the cause is in the reason) or `empty`. Applying says nothing about compiling or passing tests.
- **overlap**: how many of the commit's files `callgrind-20g` changed since the merge base.
- **status**: `WORTH` applies as text (`git apply`, not a three-way merge) to the pinned base, compiles with no warnings, and the tests that exercise the change pass there. A row whose tests cannot run on this machine is WORTH only with the gap named in its reason. `ADAPT` is worth having but does not apply as text (it needs a three-way merge, a port or a rewrite), so it is rewritten here; the reason says what the port changes. `SKIP` is not worth having here, or is superseded, or its premise is absent; the reason says which. `HAVE` means this branch already has the change. `OPEN` is not judged yet; a reopened row keeps its earlier verdict in the why column as `reopened from SKIP: ...`.

## Decisions

- **Effects.** Each effect-soundness fix is ported into the demand-driven model (`registry/demand.rs`), starting with a fixture. Master's effects engine is not migrated.
- **Arena and typed ids.** Not migrated. A fix from these rows is ported only where its bug reproduces on this branch.
- **Validation and dump tooling.** SKIP: the branch keeps its own gates (`xtask`) and fixtures. Rows that are fixes rather than tooling keep the verdict their reason gives.
- **php-semantics track.** Master deferred the php-semantics research track and the PHP catalog at row 176 (`f8f0031d`). The SKIPs that rested on that deferral are reopened and re-judged one by one on their merits.
- **PHP-version syntax.** Rows 105, 106 and 107 are ported together and ship opt-in, not on by default.
- **Gate store.** Row 119 is split, not migrated as is: snapshots stay in the shared store, and the scratch paths move to each checkout's `.corpus-runs/scratch`, which `.gitignore` must ignore.
